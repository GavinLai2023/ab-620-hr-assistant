# Contoso New-Hire Onboarding Checklist

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## Before day 1
Bring photo ID (passport or driver licence), your bank account details for payroll, and your tax file number declaration. Return the signed employment contract to the HR Officer at least 3 working days before your start date.

## Day 1
Arrive at 9:00 AM and check in at reception. The IT Helpdesk issues your laptop, sets up your Microsoft 365 account, and enrols you in multi-factor authentication. Reception issues your building access card; a lost card costs $20 to replace.

## First week
Complete three mandatory trainings in the learning portal before the end of your first week:
- Code of Conduct (45 minutes)
- Information Security Basics (60 minutes)
- Workplace Health and Safety (30 minutes)

Your manager sets up a 30-minute welcome meeting to agree your first-month goals.

## First 30 days
Your manager holds a probation check-in meeting by day 30. Raise any payroll or contract questions with the HR Officer, and any laptop or account issues with the IT Helpdesk. Annual leave can be requested from your first month, but leave in the first 30 days needs manager approval in advance.

## Contacts
- HR Officer: contracts, payroll, probation
- IT Helpdesk: laptop, accounts, access card faults
- Your manager: goals, schedule, probation check-in
