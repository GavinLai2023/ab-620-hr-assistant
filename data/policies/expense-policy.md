# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

## What is reimbursable
Business travel, client meals, conference fees, and work-related training are reimbursable. Personal expenses, alcohol outside client meals, and fines are not.

## Limits
Single expenses above 500 USD require manager pre-approval. Daily meal allowance during travel is 60 USD. Hotel stays are capped at 200 USD per night unless a conference rate applies.

## Submitting claims
Submit claims within 30 days of the expense with itemized receipts. Claims without receipts are limited to 25 USD per item. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle.

## Travel booking
Flights and hotels must be booked through the Contoso travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.
