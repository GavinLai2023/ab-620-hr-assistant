# Contoso Annual Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## Entitlement
All full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on contracted hours. Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month from the first day of employment.

## Requesting leave
Requests must be submitted through the HR Assistant or the HR portal at least 5 working days before the first day of leave. Requests longer than 10 consecutive working days require 4 weeks notice. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days.

## Carry-over
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March. Days beyond 5 are forfeited unless HR grants a written exception.

## Cancelling approved leave
Approved leave can be cancelled up to 2 working days before it starts. Cancellations inside that window need manager approval and are handled case by case.

## Public holidays
Contoso observes 11 public holidays per year. These do not count against annual leave.
