# Contoso Remote Work Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Roles that require on-site presence, such as facilities and reception, are excluded.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable on Teams between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone.

## Equipment
Contoso provides a laptop and one external monitor. A one-time home office allowance of 300 USD is available after probation. Internet costs are not reimbursed.

## Security
Remote work requires the Contoso VPN and multi-factor authentication. Company data must not be stored on personal devices.

## Working abroad
Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year requires written approval from HR and Legal.
