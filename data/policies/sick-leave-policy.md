# Contoso Sick Leave Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Owner: Human Resources.

## Entitlement
Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out.

## Notification
Notify your manager before 9:30 AM on the first day of absence by Teams message or phone. Absences of 3 or more consecutive working days require a medical certificate submitted to HR within 5 working days of returning.

## Extended illness
After 10 paid days are used, employees may apply for unpaid sick leave or short-term disability. Contact HR for eligibility.

## Caring for dependents
Up to 3 of the 10 sick days per year may be used to care for an ill child, partner, or parent.
