# Contoso Careers FAQ

Answers for people applying to work at Contoso. This page covers public information only.

## About Contoso

Contoso is a mid-sized company with teams in Engineering, Finance, HR and Sales. We hire for full-time roles and a small number of internships each year.

## How do I apply?

1. Find an open role on the Contoso careers page.
2. Select **Apply** and upload your CV. A cover letter is optional.
3. You get an email confirming we received your application.

You can apply for up to three roles at the same time.

## What does the hiring process look like?

| Stage | What happens | Typical time |
|---|---|---|
| Application review | A recruiter reads your CV | Up to 10 working days |
| Phone screen | 30-minute call with a recruiter | 1 week after review |
| Interviews | 2 to 4 interviews with the team, online or in the office | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Offer | The recruiter calls you, then sends a written offer | Within 5 working days of the last interview |

## How are interviews scheduled?

The recruiter emails you a link to pick a time. If you need to change the time, use the same link at least 24 hours before the interview. If you need adjustments for a disability, tell the recruiter and we will arrange them.

## What benefits does Contoso offer?

- Paid annual leave, starting at 20 days a year for full-time staff
- Paid sick leave
- Health insurance
- Hybrid working for most roles
- A yearly learning budget

Exact terms depend on the country and the role and are listed in the offer.

## Can I check the status of my application?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email, or contact the careers team below. We cannot share information about current employees or internal HR matters.
